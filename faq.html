Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Siso Academy. Can't find your answer? Reach out to us.

// Getting Started
How do I access the free lessons?
All our lessons are available on our Lectures page. Each video is hosted on YouTube and can be watched completely free. No sign-up or subscription required.
Do I need any prerequisites to start learning?
No! Our courses start from the basics. Each topic begins with foundational concepts and builds up to more advanced material. You can start with whichever topic interests you most.
Can I download the notes as PDF?
Yes! Our Notes page contains downloadable PDF materials for each topic. All notes are completely free. The download feature will be available soon.
// About the Content
What topics are covered?
We cover 5 main areas: Algebra (equations, polynomials, inequalities), Calculus (limits, differentiation, integration), Geometry (shapes, proofs, coordinate geometry), Statistics (probability, distributions), and Number Theory (primes, modular arithmetic).
Are the lessons suitable for exam preparation?
Absolutely! Our step-by-step approach is designed to build genuine understanding that helps with exams. Many students have improved their results after following our lessons. We focus on concepts rather than memorisation tricks.
How long is each video lesson?
Video lengths vary by topic and complexity. Most lessons range from 30 minutes to 1 hour, covering the topic in depth with worked examples. You can always pause and rewatch any section.
Do you have practice problems?
Yes! Check out our Practice page for exercises with solutions. We have problems ranging from easy to hard, so you can test your understanding at any level.
// Costs & Access
Is everything really free?
Yes! All videos, notes, and practice problems are completely free. We believe everyone deserves access to quality mathematics education. Siso Academy will always be free.
How do you sustain the free content?
Siso Academy is supported by the YouTube Partner Program and generous supporters who believe in free education. If you'd like to support us, subscribe to our YouTube channel and share our content.
Is there any premium content?
No. All our content remains free. There are no paywalls, subscriptions, or premium tiers. This is our promise to the community.
// Technical
Which devices can I use to watch?
Our videos work on any device with internet access - computers, tablets, and smartphones. You can also download videos for offline viewing through the YouTube app.
Can I suggest a topic or request a video?
Absolutely! We'd love to hear your suggestions. Reach out through our contact information or comment on YouTube videos. We regularly create content based on viewer requests.

Still have questions?

We're here to help. Reach out and we'll get back to you.
