// Our Story

How It Started

Siso Academy began with a simple observation: many students struggle with mathematics not because they're "bad at math," but because they've never been taught in a way that makes sense to them.

Traditional education often moves too fast, skips crucial steps, and relies on memorisation rather than understanding. We wanted to create something different — content that actually helps people get math.

What started as a few videos on YouTube has grown into a full library of lessons, notes, and practice problems. And this is just the beginning.
